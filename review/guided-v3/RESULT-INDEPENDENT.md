# Independent visual realism result — guided v3

**FAIL — do not deploy this as a completed, physically convincing windshield installation.**

Asset: assets/install-guided-v3.mp4. Parent reports approved generation cost $0.96. This review makes no additional spending request or media change.

## Evidence inspected

All four 4fps contact sheets covering 0.00–7.75 seconds; individual sampled frames 009,016,025,032; and fresh native 1920×1080 extractions at 3.75s and 7.75s, saved as full-3.75.png and full-7.75.png in this directory. Source is 24fps. I did not observe full-speed playback and do not claim to have inspected every source frame.

## Material defect

The pane does not reach a convincing fitted position. At 3.75s its upper-right corner is visibly raised/forward relative to the roof edge, producing an angular projecting corner and a pronounced strip along the right side. At 7.75s this remains visible: lower and left edges look close to the opening, but the right/top corner still stands away rather than aligning into the surrounding opening. The final sampled frames retain that displaced geometry while the technicians begin changing/moving their grip on the suction lifters. The scene therefore reads as a skewed or insufficiently seated pane, not a successful completed placement.

The sampled sequence also gives the pane a changing right-edge profile during descent; whether this is generated deformation or unintended tilt cannot be certified from stills alone. The unresolved final fit is itself sufficient to fail this visual gate. Do not conceal it with a crop or a claim that the attractive overall scene proves correct installation.

## Improvements that do not override the failure

- The initial opening is visibly empty; no obvious second installed windshield is present beneath the lifted pane.
- The black pane perimeter is much cleaner than the previous attempt, without its ragged lower border.
- Paired blue suction tools and hand positions are broadly continuous and understandable in the reviewed frames. I did not identify a comparably clear separate hand/tool-contact blocker.
- No wipers are visibly obstructing the opening, and the hood protection is retained.

This is an independent visual plausibility assessment, not mechanical-expert certification. The primary finding is the visible unresolved pane alignment/seating at the end, not a general judgment of the technicians, equipment model or real-world installation procedure.
