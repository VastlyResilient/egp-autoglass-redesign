# Windsor rebuild — desktop captured-state comparison

Compared EGP review/rebuild-desktop/board-0.jpg, board-9.jpg and board-18.jpg with Windsor research/windsor-exact/board-0.jpg, board-8.jpg, board-16.jpg and board-24.jpg. Enlarged EGP frames 04, 08, 20 and 23 and reference frames 07 and 22. Both enlarged sets are 1440×900. These are selected scroll samples, not synchronized motion recordings.

## Concrete findings

1. **P1 readability: assembly heading is white over pale glass.** EGP 04/08 show the white section label and “Complete auto glass service, brought to you” on a very light windshield/background. Parts of the heading approach white-on-white. This needs a darker local scrim or dark text; preserving the reference typography does not require preserving white when the substituted image is much lighter. Service-card text itself is clear.
2. **P2 animation geometry: the orbit transition collapses into a heap.** EGP20 shows most cards clustered and overlapping in a narrow vertical stack, with an isolated car tile at left. Reference18 shows a clean spreading arc before the circle forms. The current interpolation visibly loses the reference’s orderly spacing. Reconstruct the arc/curvature interpolation and inspect multiple intermediate progress values, including reverse.
3. **P2 reference composition gap: the final orbit is sparse and uneven.** EGP23 uses eight separated items, including repeated imagery and transparent exploded-car cutouts. Reference22 uses a dense ring of opaque photographic tiles around a larger visual opening. The cutouts break the shared card silhouette, while duplicated car assets make the repetition conspicuous. Existing assets can be placed on opaque backgrounds and cropped consistently; this finding does not require new paid generation.

## What improved, and what remains unverified

The left navigation capsule, centered branding, right contact pill, smaller section headings, split section introductions, four-card assembly and horizontal feature sequence are recognizably closer to the supplied reference than the earlier rejected design. Current service-card spacing and typography are readable in their hold frame. This does not establish exact measured or motion fidelity: section entry progress differs across supplied captures, the hero is intentionally static while film is unresolved, and the orbit difference is significant.

Source follow-up confirms the menu now has viewport-bounded scrolling and body lock; proof travel is being revised dynamically, and the parent reports orbit CTA inert/eager hero repairs. These require responsive interaction verification, not a desktop board assertion. The new source avoids the rejected “No invented promises” customer heading.

No new image/video generation is recommended by this review. Fix contrast and orbit geometry first, then compare matched progress captures and real forward/reverse sequences. Short-height and responsive capture review is still pending.
