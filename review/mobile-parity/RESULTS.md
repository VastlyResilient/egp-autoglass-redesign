# EGP mobile motion verification — 2026-10-15

All 12 routes were tested in a real browser using same-origin responsive iframe viewports at 320×568, 390×844, and 844×390. The homepage motion families were also compared at 820×1180 and 1440×900. This is responsive browser evidence, not physical iPhone/Android or native touch-event testing.

## Coverage
- 114 scroll text reveals and 47 native disclosures per all-route run; text reached full visibility and disclosures opened.
- Film sampled forward through frames 0–191 and back to 0; six staged HTML beats retained.
- Four service cards, four horizontal image-fill panels, proof rail, and eight-image closing ring sampled at nine forward/reverse positions.
- Town ticker and rotating globe pause/resume observed; reduced-motion town wrapping and eight linked orbit images inspected.
- Mobile menu opened, Escape dismissed it; keyboard Tab advanced between visible closing-image links.
- Final 320/390 runs report no document overflow. Tablet and desktop home retain the same motion families.

## Repairs
Constrained narrow-screen globe; separated mobile orbit text from moving images; fitted landscape cards and ring; strengthened expanding-image text contrast; added ambient pause/resume; preserved all towns and orbit destinations under reduced motion; enlarged service-link targets; added touch-active feedback and excluded clipped orbit links from keyboard focus.

## Boundaries
No claim of exhaustive physical-device, native touch, browser-engine, third-party form submission, or operating-system low-power-mode testing. Intermediate screenshot review is complemented by measured scene samples, not a continuous hardware recording. Independent review is in INDEPENDENT.md.
