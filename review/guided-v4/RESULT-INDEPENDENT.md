## Blocking ending defect

At 6.5s the pane appears seated and stationary relative to the roof, pillars and dashboard. From approximately 6.5–7.75s both paired suction lifters move upward across that apparently stationary glass, with the cups continuing to appear pressed against its surface. In the native images, the left upper cup moves from approximately y410 to y325 and its lower cup from approximately y490 to y400; the glass boundaries remain essentially in place. The right pair also shifts upward/inward. The hands follow the moving tools into the final target pose. The sampled frames do not show a readable lift-off and reattachment that explains this contact-point change.

This reads as the tools sliding/morphing toward the prescribed ending rather than maintaining their fixed grip through seating. It is not merely a harmless hand adjustment: the cup contact positions themselves change. Releasing and removing tools after seating could be plausible, but that action must be visually legible; it is not legible in the delivered ending.

## Disposition

Do not mark the full clip accepted. The main lowering/seating portion is a stronger candidate than v3. A separately trimmed ending around the seated stage might avoid the later sliding, but would need its own frame/full-speed review and endpoint hold assessment; this report does not automatically approve a trim. A smooth-looking final still does not validate the transition into it. This is visual plausibility assessment, not mechanical-expert certification.
