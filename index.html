Do you come to me?

Yes. EGP offers free mobile auto glass service in Meriden and surrounding Central Connecticut towns. Contact the team to confirm your location.

Should my glass be repaired or replaced?

EGP can examine the damage and recommend an option for your windshield, window or mirror. Ask the team about your specific vehicle and damage.

Can you help with insurance?

EGP assists with glass claims and coordinates with your insurer. Confirm coverage, deductible and any cost before scheduling.

How do I get a quote?

Use the online quote service, review the details, then arrange your service. You can also call 203-213-1257.

Do you match written quotes?

EGP states that it will match any written quote. Contact the team with your quote to discuss the details.
